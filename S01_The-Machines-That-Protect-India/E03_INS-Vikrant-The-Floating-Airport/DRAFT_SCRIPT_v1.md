## Summary of the episode in plain words
INS Vikrant is India's first home-designed aircraft carrier: an airport that floats. It solves three problems: a ski-jump ramp gets jets airborne from a short deck, arrester wires stop them when they land, and an on-board "city" of power, hangars, lifts and crew keeps the airport running. India designed and built the ship but still imports its carrier fighter (the French Rafale M is being trialled) and does not yet use a catapult. The episode compares launch methods without ranking anyone, shows a clearly fictional crisis, uses the planning fallacy as its human idea, and ends by asking who will build the aircraft for the ship.
