## Distinguishing Akash variants (for accuracy)
- Akash Prime (2022 flight test per PIB year-end release) is described as having an indigenous active RF seeker, so the seeker alone does **not** distinguish Akash-NG from Akash Prime. The episode therefore says only what the MoD says about Akash-NG and does not claim the seeker is unique to it.

## Not publicly documented — must not be shown or invented

## Wording notes
