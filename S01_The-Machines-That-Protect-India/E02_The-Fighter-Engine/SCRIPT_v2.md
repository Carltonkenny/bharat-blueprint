## Things I decided or changed, and why

1. **No earlier Ep2 script existed in the files.** The older `EPISODES.md` Ep2 was a missile episode and the master reference described a 2:15–2:30 format with another voice. Your new decisions replace that, so this script is fresh, built on the fighter-engine arc and guardrails in the Claude reference pack. I have not edited those older files.
2. **Marut fits in one line.** The pack gives it a full historical section; at 256 words it only fits in the hook. It is fair (not called a failure) and sourced, but if you want more of it, something else has to give.
3. **The exact three engineering ideas** are squeeze (pressure), heat (temperature and materials), proof (tests and reliability). Tolerances, FADEC and afterburners are left out.
4. **India's "cannot yet" stops at Tejas on American engines.** The two future engines (F414, Safran–GTRE) are both unfinished deals in public reporting (P01, P02), so I put them on screen as "in negotiation / announced, approval not confirmed" and keep them out of the narration. If a signature lands before render, I will update the card.
5. **I left out the 48.5 kN and 81–83 kN figures** because only media and Wikipedia carry them (P03, ledger A/S06).
6. **Comparison names no countries on the ladder** and puts no flags on it, only engine families as examples. The narration says "China joined them later" because it is a statement about timing, not a rank; it rests on secondary sources (S16) and can be dropped if you prefer.
7. **Blend of footage:** the pack asks for real footage and photos as evidence. Because this episode is the code-drawn blueprint look you chose, I use none, so there is no Evidence Hold.
8. **Pronunciation to check in the audition:** "crore", "Kaveri", "Tejas", "Marut".
