## C. Challenge pass (try to disprove each number before lock)

- "Over three thousand test hours": S01 says 3,217. Holds. The 2012 reply says 2,200 h and a DRDO press clipping says 73 flight hours against PIB's 57 h in 2012. The numbers differ by date, so only the latest PIB figure is used.
- "Nine prototype engines": S01 and S02 agree (plus four core engines).
- "About two thousand crore rupees": S01 gives ₹2,035.56 cr spent as of 2021. S02 gives ₹1,996 cr in 2012. Holds.
- "Fell short of the thrust Tejas needed": S03 and S04. The Tejas requirement has changed over time. The 2021 text says the final-clearance configuration needs more thrust than the intended engine. The script says only "fell short".
- "Tejas flies on American engines": S07 and S08. True for Mk1 and Mk1A. The Mk2 is planned on the F414 (P01), so "flies" is present tense and Mk1/Mk1A only.
- "Both can be true": opinion, from the series pack. Keep.
- "The Marut never got the engine it was designed for": S09. Primary page still wanted (see S09).
- Not claimed anywhere: that India has a fielded indigenous modern fighter engine; that Kaveri was a failure; that any foreign supplier acted in bad faith.
