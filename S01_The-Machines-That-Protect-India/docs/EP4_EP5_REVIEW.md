## EP4 — How does a missile find its target?

**Novelty fill (illusion-of-novelty skill)**
- **Reveal:** in 2026 the government described Mission Sudarshan Chakra as a multi-level, three-layered missile-defence system. PIB, PRID 2272327. VERIFIED (snippet; read the full page before scripting).
- **Contrast:** "We think defence is about firing. The hard part is seeing, deciding and steering in seconds."
- **Urgency:** a new weapon-system complex was inaugurated at DRDL Hyderabad on 12 June 2026 (cited via Wikipedia to PIB: TO VERIFY from PIB).
- **Proof:** PIB pages above, plus PIB relid 22539 for the 1983 programme.

**Pretext (why people should care):** a missile programme that began in 1983 under technology denial is now the base for a national shield. Citizens pay for it, live under it, and rarely see how it works.

**Non-engineering takeaways (all sourced)**
1. The programme began in July 1983. PIB relid 22539, VERIFIED.
2. Akash was sanctioned at ₹95.34 crore in 1983 and ₹548.45 crore by 2007. Completion slipped from July 1995 to December 2007. PIB relid 22539, VERIFIED.
3. Mission Sudarshan Chakra was announced on 15 Aug 2025 and aims at a shield by 2035. "Aims" wording only. PIB PRID 2205873, VERIFIED.

**Three ideas, each with a why-line, an everyday example and before / during / after** (concept level only, no ranges, speeds, seeker or warhead detail)
1. **Sense.** Why: "to hit something you must first know where it is." Example: shouting in a valley and timing the echo. Radar sweep, echo pulses.
2. **Decide.** Why: "knowing is not enough, you must aim where it will be." Example: catching a ball, you move to where it will land. Animated intercept triangle.
3. **Steer.** Why: "a decision is useless until the body obeys." Example: steering a bicycle. Fins deflect with a spring settle.

**Structure:** hook → pretext → 3 ideas → India can / cannot yet → fair look at the three ways to sense (radar, heat, light), no rankings → fictional what-if → hindsight bias → closing question. About 4:30.
**India can:** design, build and flight-test missile families (Trishul, Akash, Nag named in PIB 22539). **Cannot yet:** a complete national shield; it is a 2035 aim.
**Behaviour idea:** hindsight bias. The path from denial to design looks obvious now, so we underrate how uncertain it was.
**Fresh closing line (not the banned one):** to be written with the script; target shape: "A shield is a chain. Which link comes last?"

**Look and motion (new bar)**
- Opening: a parts list where items cross out one by one, then a 1983 to 2035 timeline with a moving playhead and counting years.
- Radar sweep with echo pulses; a missile flying a curved path with a seeker cone; the intercept triangle drawing itself; fins deflecting with spring physics and a thump on impact.
- Blueprint set: generic missile in top, side and front views with projection lines, and an **exploded view** (seeker, guidance computer, motor, fins), concept level and labelled "conceptual, not to scale".
- Idle motion everywhere: rotating radars, drifting particles, blinking lights. No drawing sits still.

**Novelty fill**
- **Reveal:** exports hit a record ₹38,424 crore, announced this year. **Contrast:** "India buys its weapons" versus a country selling to more than 80 others. **Urgency:** the 2029 targets were restated on 3 Nov 2026. **Proof:** PIB.
- **Honest caveat built into the script:** exports are about a fifth of production, and production is about a quarter of the defence budget. A percentage from a tiny base sounds huge, and the film says so. That is the **small-base effect**, the behaviour idea.

**Pretext:** "Who actually builds all this?" The episode follows one missile to the factory floor.
**Three ideas (industry, each plain and with an example)**
1. **Many small makers make one machine.** Example: a car built from hundreds of workshops. Supplier web grows node by node.
2. **Nothing ships until it passes.** Example: school exams. Callback to Ep2's "proof" idea.
3. **Making one versus making a thousand.** Example: baking one cake versus a bakery. Counters and a moving production line.

**Structure:** hook → pretext → 3 ideas → can / cannot yet (what is built here, what is still bought, per PIB) → fair look at buy / licence-build / co-develop (callback to Ep2's cake) → fictional what-if (a spare part is needed on a Sunday) → small-base effect → recap of all five episodes → closing question. About 4:30.
**Look and motion:** counters ticking up; 2014 and FY 2025-26 bars growing on one scale with a pen trail; a factory-floor plan with a conveyor and moving parts; an abstract corridor map; a five-episode recap strip where each thumbnail animates in turn; a magnifier inset on one small part.
