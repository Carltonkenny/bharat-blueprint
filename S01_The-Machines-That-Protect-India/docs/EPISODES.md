## EP2 — FROM EMBARGO TO ENGINEERING (missiles)
**Logline:** a country that was once denied the technology to build guided missiles built a programme, then a family of systems, then a system like Akash-NG.
**Hook scenario:** a 1980s drafting room: a blank sheet and a list of components marked "not available". (Visual: parts crossing out one by one.) Not a real person or event; labelled as an illustration.
**THEN (0:15–0:45)**
- IGMDP launched 1983 under Dr APJ Abdul Kalam at DRDO. VERIFIED: PIB (relid 35689, relid 22539).
- Programme covered Prithvi, Akash, Nag, Trishul, and Agni as a technology demonstrator (re-entry). VERIFIED: PIB relid 35689, PrintRelease 85986.
- MoD itself lists "denial of critical technologies, import embargoes" among reasons for delays. VERIFIED: PIB relid 22539.
- Sanctioned costs rose between 1983 and 2007 (Akash 95.34 cr → 548.45 cr; Trishul 27.16 → 289.15). VERIFIED: PIB relid 22539. Use as an honest "it was hard" beat, no blame.
**NOW (0:45–1:30)**
- Akash family to Akash-NG: indigenous RF seeker, solid rocket motor; UET completed Dec 2025 (reuse Ep1 facts). VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2207971.
- DRDO's own site lists Agni and Prithvi series, Pinaka, Akash, radars and electronic warfare systems as indigenous successes. VERIFIED: drdo.gov.in home page.
- Engineering explainer: "how a guided missile finds its target" at concept level only (sense → decide → steer), force and control-surface arrows, no seeker internals or guidance specifics. ENGINEERING EXPLANATION.
**NEXT (1:30–1:55)**
- Mission Sudarshan Chakra, announced from the Red Fort on 15 Aug 2025: aims at an indigenous, comprehensive national security shield by 2035; research, design and manufacture in India. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2205873 and PressNote 155066. Word as "aims to".
**Reveal (1:55–2:20):** the embargo sheet from the hook now filled with drawn components; callback line; title.
**Signature blueprint set pieces:** parts crossing out; a 1983→2025 timeline drawn as a dimension chain; a guided-missile sense/decide/steer loop with force arrows (orange only for motor heat).
**New drawing objects:** drafting room/desk, component list, timeline chain, generic missile with control surfaces, loop diagram.
**Premium moments:** the timeline "inks" through 40 years with a hot pen trail; key numbers (1983, 2025, 2035) in 64 px+ mono callouts.

## EP3 — THE FIGHTER AND THE ENGINE (combat aircraft)
**Logline:** designing a fighter is one thing; building the airframe, electronics and the engine inside it is the real test.
**Hook scenario:** a hangar at night, an aircraft under tarp, a single question: "Who made every part inside it?"
**THEN (0:15–0:50)**
- India's early jet fighters were largely acquired or built under licence (TO VERIFY with HAL/IAF histories). HAL HF-24 Marut as India-designed early fighter (TO VERIFY).
- Tejas programme start in the 1980s (TO VERIFY: ADA/PIB).
**NOW (0:50–1:45)**
- Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA) designed and developed Tejas; completed 25 years of Tejas flight (event of 4 Jan 2026); more than 5,600 flight trials; "capability and capacity to build fighter aircraft indigenously". VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2211249.
- Tejas type certified; IAF has operationalised Mk1. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2007465.
- Mk1A: advanced mission computer, Digital Flight Control Computer (DFCC, flown 19 Feb 2024 on prototype LSP7), smart multi-function displays, AESA radar, advanced self-protection jammer. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2007465.
- Industry shift: first centre-fuselage assembly for Tejas Mk1A handed to HAL by VEM Technologies on 30 May 2025, a first for a major sub-assembly. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2132890.
- Engineering explainer: "how a jet engine makes thrust" (intake → compressor → combustion → turbine → nozzle; cut-away; afterburner glow in orange), then "why fly-by-wire needs a computer" at concept level. ENGINEERING EXPLANATION.
**NEXT (1:45–2:10)**
- AMCA: the Raksha Mantri approved the programme execution model on 27 May 2025; ADA to run it through industry partnership; public and private firms can bid alone or as consortia; the bidder must be an Indian company. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2131528.
- Independence Day 2025: the PM urged Indian innovators to develop jet engines within India. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2210154. Word as a call/goal.
**Reveal (2:10–2:30):** the aircraft's skeleton turns into its supply chain: airframe, electronics, engine; callback; title.
**Signature set pieces:** orthographic aircraft in three views with projection lines; exploded view; turbine cut-away with airflow arrows and a heat gradient (orange only here); a wiring/data "fly-by-wire" loop.
**New objects:** fighter in 3 views (generic enough to avoid unverified dimensions), turbine stages, AESA panel, flight-control loop.
**Premium moments:** the engine's air path glows from cyan to orange as it heats; exploded view re-assembles on the narration word "indigenous".
**Avoid:** thrust figures, speeds, radar ranges, weapon loads, comparisons with other fighters.
**TO VERIFY before scripting:** Marut, early licence production, Tejas start date, engine status (Kaveri) from ADA/GTRE/PIB.

## EP5 — FROM BUYING TO BUILDING (the industrial base)
**Logline:** the part you don't see in any weapon: a country-sized factory floor.
**Hook scenario:** an empty factory shift-board that fills with names of small companies.
**THEN (0:15–0:45)**
- Indigenous defence production was ₹46,429 crore in 2014-15. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2191937.
- Defence exports were under ₹1,000 crore in 2014. VERIFIED (snippet): PIB "Defence Atmanirbharta" backgrounder, 20 Nov 2025.
**NOW (0:45–1:40)**
- Production: ₹1,27,434 crore in FY 2023-24 (+174% vs 2014-15); record ₹1.54 lakh crore in FY 2024-25. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2191937.
- Exports: record ₹23,622 crore in FY 2024-25. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2116612 and 2191937.
- 2024-25: record 193 contracts worth ₹2,09,050 crore; 177 (about 92%) to domestic industry (₹1,68,922 crore). VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2191937.
- About 16,000 MSMEs; 788 industrial licences to 462 companies. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2191937.
- Up to 74% FDI via the automatic route; iDEX for innovation; defence industrial corridors in UP and Tamil Nadu with over ₹9,145 crore of investment and 289 MoUs. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2205873 and New India Samachar Oct 2025.
**NEXT (1:40–2:05)**
- Stated aim: ₹3 lakh crore in production and ₹50,000 crore in exports by 2029. VERIFIED (snippet): PIB "Make in India Powers Defence Growth".
- Sudarshan Chakra Mission aims for a national security shield by 2035. VERIFIED: PIB PRID 2205873.
**Reveal (2:05–2:20):** every previous episode's system fades into the factory web; callback; series title card.
**Signature set pieces:** counters that tick up in 64 px+ mono; a before/after bar drawn as a dimension chain on the same scale; a map of corridors (abstract outline, no site claims); a supplier-web that links back to Episodes 1–4.
**New objects:** factory floor plan, bar/timeline charts, abstract map nodes, series recap strip.
**Premium moments:** the 2014→2025 bars grow in one sweep with a hot pen trail; recap strip shows all five titles inked in.
**Avoid:** per-company claims, rankings, and mixing financial years without labelling them.
