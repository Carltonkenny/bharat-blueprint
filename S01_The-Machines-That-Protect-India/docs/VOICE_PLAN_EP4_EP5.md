## How to spend as little as possible
1. Tone test first: one passage only (about 330 credits) in the new Unseen Narration tags (`[calm]` base, `[serious]` on the stakes lines); you listen and approve.
2. Generate Episode 4 alone, listen, then Episode 5. One take per passage, no variations.
3. Passages are stored in each episode folder: narration/voice-plan.json (text with pauses, three clips each). Splitting and the 1.12x tempo are done locally and cost nothing.
4. After the voice is measured the timeline re-times itself and the film is re-rendered (local, free). Sound effects and the loudness mix are built locally too.
5. If credits are short, record only the hook and the closing (the first and last two passages) and use captions for the rest.
