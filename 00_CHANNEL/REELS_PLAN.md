# Reels, Shorts and teaser ads for The Machines That Protect India (plan, nothing built yet)

Written 2026-11-04 after research. Sources are listed at the bottom. Nothing here has been rendered or published.

## What the research says (and how sure it is)
- Instagram says watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach are the top Reels signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025, via secondary write-ups). The first 1 to 3 seconds decide whether people stay.
- Reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to non-followers; longer ones cannot. Length advice conflicts: 15-30 s for completion, 45-60 s for views in one 2026 analysis. Treat length as a test, not a rule.
- Meta's own 9:16 safe zone: keep key text out of the top 14% (~270 px), bottom 35% (~670 px) and sides 6% (~65 px) of 1080x1920. This is stricter than the ig-reel skill's default (y 230 to 1440), so use Meta's numbers.
- YouTube Shorts: up to 3 minutes. Studio's "viewed vs swiped away" is the number that judges the first 2 seconds. Seamless loops raise replays.
- Repurposing one long video into 5 to 15 vertical cuts is the standard faceless-channel move.
- Reels are often watched muted first: the hook has to work as text.

## Honest limits
- Our films are 16:9 (1280x720). A vertical cut is a new composition, not a crop.
- Hook scorer (ig-reel) on six drafts: best was 57 (OK), worst 39. Top: "In 1983 a missile cost ninety-five crore. By 2007 it cost five hundred and forty-eight." None reached STRONG; the work is to add stakes and a specific viewer.

## Proposed build
Per episode: 1 hook reel (20-35 s, teases the full film), 1 idea reel (35-50 s, one engineering idea with its everyday example), 1 stat/behaviour reel (20-30 s). 15 reels in total. Same cuts exported for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and a 9-12 s teaser for boosting.
Layout: 1080x1920 canvas, the 16:9 scene clip centred inside the safe box, hook card above it, word-timed captions below it, series tag and "full film" CTA. All text inside Meta's safe zone.
Voice: reuse the existing Daniel clips (no credits). New voice only for a 5-8 s spoken hook if a cut needs one.
Facts: only from each episode's source ledger; unverified items worded "announced/reported" or dropped.

## Open decisions for the user
1. Series/channel names and handles.
2. Letterboxed vertical (fast) or full vertical re-layout of the best scenes (slower, better looking).
3. Reels only, or also boosted ads (check Meta's rules for social-issue/political ads in India first; not verified).
4. One keyword CTA, and where it sends people.
