## Long vs short
Both, but mostly long. AIR Media-Tech (18,000 channels, a correlation, not proof): best results with Shorts at 25-40% of uploads; long-form results fall once Shorts pass 55%; channels that shifted hard to Shorts did poorly. Metricool's data (secondary): about 5 Shorts a month went with higher long-form views. Shorts and long-form run on separate recommendation systems, so use the "related video" link and measure.
- YouTube: keep Shorts at or under 40% of uploads. Week 1: 5 long films, 3 Shorts. Steady state: 1 Short per 2 long videos.
- Instagram Reels is a separate platform: post the dozen below within two weeks, then 3 a week. This is my suggestion to test, not a researched number.
