## What I saw (frame timestamps from the draft)
1. **Frames are too sparse.** At 0:05, 1:53, 2:53 and 5:00 one object sits in the middle of a mostly empty frame. The brief asks for a world with layered depth: a hero plus its context drawings, rails with leader-lined labels, and motion.
2. **Placeholders are on screen.** At 0:40 two large pale-blue rounded boxes with an "I" in each (the puzzle pieces of E01 P02) look like unfinished UI. At 1:16 the cook scene shows only a tiny orange dot and two arrows pointing at nothing: the cook figure, stove and flame are missing, so "FUEL + OXYGEN = HEAT" explains nothing.
3. **The figure layer is not visible.** None of the eight samples contains a line figure, yet E01 has cook, cyclist, pressure cooker and two engineers (P02 to P05). The brief gives about 12% of screen time to characters.
4. **Labels float.** "PRESSURE UP" (1:53) and "CHAMBER STARVED" (4:16) sit in the top-right with no leader line to the part, and two stray horizontal lines at 1:53 point at nothing. QC B2/B3 pass on size and overlap, but a label that does not touch its part fails the brief ("placed beside the component they identify").
5. **Low contrast and small text.** The source chip "ISRO, 6 JUL 2026" (3:36) and the ruler words TEST / THRUST / QUALIFIED are barely readable against the paper; HUD labels are tiny at 1080p. Raise contrast to at least 4.5:1 and the source chip to at least 1.9% of frame height.
6. **The same hero image repeats.** 4:16 and 5:00 (and the earlier 5.07 / 8.04 stills) show the same chamber from the same angle with the same flame disc. Use the camera plan in `CAMERA_AND_ZONES_S02.md` (orbit, push-in, dolly), the exploded view, the clipping-plane cutaway and a particle flow so each beat shows something new.
7. **The "flame" is a flat disc** (grey with an orange ring). Make the combustion read as combustion: seeded particle flow from the chamber through the throat and out of the nozzle, amber at the throat, cooling passages in cyan; and the failing version in the fictional passage should visibly starve.
8. **Frame gates already failed on this draft:** F3 pops at frames 460, 476, 4040, 4812, 6663, 7638 and F4 flicker at frames 53, 343, 7155, 7156, 7172. Fix the cause, do not widen the gate.

## Add as measurable gates (log them in QC_BAR_S02.md under "reviewer additions"; do not weaken existing ones)
- **R1 density:** every `mechanism`, `reveal` and `payoff` beat contains at least 4 distinct drawn elements besides the HUD (hero, context drawing, rail label with leader line, moving flow or counter), and at most 1 beat in 10 may have only a single object.
- **R2 leader lines:** every label in RAIL_L/RAIL_R is joined by a visible leader line to its part (scene report: label has `anchorId` and a line segment ending inside the anchor's box).
- **R3 figures present:** every brief row that names a figure or prop (cook, cyclist, cooker, engineers, ...) shows it for at least 60% of that beat (scene report lists `figure:*` objects per beat).
- **R4 no placeholder shapes:** no filled rounded rectangle larger than 3% of the frame without text or an icon inside; no arrow whose head ends in empty space (arrow must end at an object or a label).
- **R5 variety:** within one passage, no two consecutive beats may share the same hero camera pose (azimuth, elevation and scale all within 3%).
- **R6 legibility:** all text contrast at least 4.5:1; HUD and chip text at least 1.9% of frame height; number-card main text at least 4% of frame height.

## Addendum (second look, same draft, frames at the five character beats 2.04, 2.06, 3.01, 4.01, 5.01 and the timeline file)
9. **The line figures are broken, not just plain.** At 0:42, 0:53, 1:09, 1:41 and 2:13 the arms are separate short strokes floating beside the body (not attached to the shoulders), there are no hands or feet, the head is far too large (head diameter about a fifth of the whole figure plus an oversized circle), and the pose never changes between samples. Props are tiny and unrecognisable: the "stove" is a line with two circles, the "pump" is a stand beside a radial cartoon, the "pressure cooker" is an empty box with three steam arcs. Each figure fills about 25% of frame height and sits left of centre with the rest of the frame empty. Fix the rig (every limb starts exactly at its parent joint, head height about one seventh of figure height, hands and feet), draw props so a first-time viewer names them in two seconds, scale the scene so the figure and prop fill at least 45% of frame height, centre the group in HERO, and make the pose animate (reach, push, pump) over the beat.
10. **Beat kinds are mis-tagged.** `timing/timeline.json` has 59 of 65 beats as `hold` and only 2 as `mech`, so the mechanism and reveal rules (and R1) are not being applied. Rule: any beat with layer H or C is `mech` unless it is only a title, stamp or number card (`number`), a `fiction` stamp beat, a `missing` list, or the `payoff`. Re-derive kinds from the brief tags and this rule, then re-run all gates.
11. **Pace is only just inside the gate.** 65 beats, 12.35 per minute, median 4.8 s (target about 15 per minute and a median near 3.5 s). The brief says to split long mechanism beats into identify / demonstrate / consequence sub-beats; only the skeleton was used. Split until the film reaches 14 to 16 beats per minute with a median of 3.5 to 4.0 s. Do not pad: each sub-beat must show a different state (a new part, a new angle, a new label or a new consequence).
12. **QC outputs look empty.** In `E01/qc/`, `scene-report.json` and `scene-gates.csv` are 0 bytes (timestamp 15:55) and `ledger-numbers.json` is 0 bytes. An empty report is not a pass. Every gate run must write a non-empty, valid file listing each gate with PASS/FAIL counts; if a gate cannot run, the run is a FAIL.
