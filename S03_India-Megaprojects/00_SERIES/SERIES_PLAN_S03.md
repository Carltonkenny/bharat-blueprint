# Series 3 plan: "India Megaprojects" (Claude, 2026-11-14). Draft for the user's approval.

## What this series is
Six films on how India builds very large civilian structures: a bridge in a gorge, a tunnel under a pass, a sea bridge, a lifting rail bridge, a transshipment port, and freight-only railways. Same channel voice and rules as S01 and S02: calm, sourced, concept level, three everyday analogies per film, honest about what is unfinished, one fictional what-if, one behaviour idea, a callback close. **Not a "denied" series**: the word is banned. Two films carry a reported dependence note (E03 deck technology and finance, E05 reliance on foreign ports as PIB words it), wording-checked as non-accusatory.

## Why these six (demand evidence is from the earlier VidIQ research, Nov 2026)
Small channels got 51K to 102K views on: "widest road tunnel", "why India plans to run only one train", "even India's PM was shocked by this machine made by Indian engineers". Megaprojects are civilian, advertiser-friendly and visual (good for the 3D heroes and line figures). Tunnel boring (S02 E06) and semiconductors (S02 E05) are already covered, so they are excluded.

## Release order (suggested, for the channel)
Production order E01 to E06 (the kit gets one bridge first). Upload order for views: E04 (Pamban: short, visual, recent), E01 (Chenab: the strongest name), E02, E03, E06, E05. Shorts: one 30 to 45 s cut per film from the "idea one" passage.

## How the series adapts the S01 and S02 spine
THE QUESTION (P01) · WHY IT MATTERS (P02, P03) · IDEA 1, 2, 3 (P04 to P06, each opens with an everyday analogy then a before/after) · INDIA = what India built and what is not yet built (P07; the slot is renamed in on-screen text if the kit allows: "WHAT IS BUILT, WHAT IS NOT YET") · THE FIELD = "measures, not a league table" (P08) · WHAT IF = FICTIONAL SCENARIO (P09) · THE HABIT = one behaviour idea (P10) · THE CLOSE = three things plus the callback question (P11).
Behaviour ideas used, one per film so none repeats: anchoring (E01), planning fallacy (E02), denominator neglect ("per what?") (E03), status quo bias (E04), demand fills capacity (E05), averages hide bottlenecks (E06). S01 used sunk cost, small-base effect and hindsight bias; S02 used its own set.
